Build your city, create jobs, and keep traffic and finances in balance.

MetroBuilder is an approachable city builder with depth:

• Roads, highways and bus lines shape traffic

• Events with real consequences
• Multiple regions with persistent cities
• Offline play — progress saved on device

Core game is free. Optional IAPs (XP / instant level) via Google Play.
